# Agency-Quality Draft

Produce content that reads like it came from a good content agency, not a first-draft generator.

## When to use

- The user hands you a brief, outline, bullet points, or a topic and wants a finished piece.
- The output is going somewhere public: blog, landing page, newsletter, sales email.
- The user says "make it agency quality", "polished", "ready to publish", or names a format.

## Get the brief straight first

Before writing, lock down (ask only if genuinely missing):
- **Audience** — who reads this, and what do they already know?
- **Goal** — inform, rank, convert, nurture? One primary goal.
- **Format & length** — blog post, landing page, email; rough word count.
- **Voice** — the brand's tone (confident, warm, technical, playful). Default to clear and confident.
- **One key takeaway** — if the reader remembers one thing, what is it?

## The agency standard

**Hook.** The first two sentences earn the third. Open with a specific tension, a concrete claim, or a sharp question — never "In today's world" or a definition of the topic.

**Structure with intent.** Every section moves the reader toward the goal. Use descriptive subheadings a skimmer can follow. Front-load value; don't bury the point in paragraph four.

**Concrete over abstract.** Numbers, examples, names, before/afters. One vivid specific beats three generic claims.

**Confident voice.** Make claims and stand behind them. Cut hedging ("might", "could arguably", "it depends"). If it depends, say on what.

**Rhythm.** Vary sentence and paragraph length. Let a short line land. Read it aloud in your head.

**Purposeful CTA.** End by pointing the reader at the next step tied to the goal — one clear action, not a menu of five.

## Quality bar before you hand it over

- Could a competitor have published the exact same piece? If yes, it's too generic — add a specific angle or opinion.
- Does every paragraph earn its place, or is some there to hit a word count? Cut the filler.
- Would the target reader forward this? If not, the hook or the takeaway is weak.
- Run it through the `humanize-ai-text` and (if it must rank) `seo-keyword-preserving-rewrite` skills as a final pass.

## Output

Deliver the finished draft. Then add a two-line note: the goal you wrote toward and the single CTA — so the user can sanity-check the direction fast.
